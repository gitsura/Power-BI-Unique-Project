# Cafe Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
The Cafe Sales Analysis Dashboard provides an interactive visualization of cafe sales data. It helps analyze sales performance, customer transactions, product performance, payment methods, and monthly sales trends.

The dashboard provides meaningful insights into business performance and helps identify popular products and sales patterns.

## Objectives
- Analyze total cafe sales.
- Understand transaction performance.
- Identify the most popular items.
- Analyze sales based on payment methods.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Compare product-wise sales performance.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard displays the following KPIs:
- **Total Sales:** 45.47K
- **Total Transactions:** 5K
- **Total Quantity:** 15K
- **Average Order Value:** 8.99

### 2. Sales Analysis by Item
A bar chart represents total sales for different cafe products:
- Salad
- Smoothie
- Sandwich
- Juice
- Cake
- Coffee
- Tea
- Cookie

### 3. Transactions by Payment Method
A pie chart displays the distribution of transactions across:
- Digital Wallet
- Credit Card
- Cash

### 4. Monthly Sales Analysis
A line chart displays monthly sales trends from January to December, helping identify sales fluctuations throughout the year.

### 5. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes an item filter to analyze sales for selected products.

## Key Insights
- Total sales reached approximately 45.47K.
- The cafe recorded approximately 5K transactions.
- Total quantity sold was approximately 15K.
- Salad recorded the highest sales among the displayed items.
- Digital Wallet, Credit Card, and Cash contributed almost equally to transactions.
- Monthly sales varied throughout the year.
